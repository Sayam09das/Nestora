# Nestora — Functional Requirements Specification

## 1. Introduction

The purpose of this Functional Requirements Specification is to define the complete, unambiguous, and testable functional requirements and business rules for **Nestora — University Hostel Management System**.

In software engineering, functional requirements define the fundamental capabilities, behaviors, and data transformations that a software system must execute to satisfy user and institutional requirements. For Nestora—an academic software engineering project for a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) curriculum—this specification establishes the authoritative baseline for system design, database architecture, user interface development, and verification testing.

All requirements detailed in this document describe **planned system behaviors** rather than completed features, aligning with the architectural scope and principles defined in [01-project-overview.md](./01-project-overview.md) and [02-project-scope.md](./02-project-scope.md).

---

## 2. User Roles and Responsibilities

Nestora establishes six distinct user roles with specific operational boundaries and responsibilities:

1. **University Administrator**: Exercises institutional oversight across all hostels within the university. Responsible for platform-level configurations, hostel provisioning, warden assignments, high-level verification and approval of admissions, fee policy authorization, escalated appeals, and university-wide operational reporting.
2. **Hostel Warden**: Exercises operational authority over their assigned hostel(s). Responsible for second-tier admission verification, room and bed allocations, leave and outing approvals, disciplinary actions, maintenance ticket assignments, emergency incident management, and hostel-level attendance and reporting.
3. **Student**: Resident user who interacts with accommodation services. Responsible for submitting admission preferences, viewing allocated room/bed details, applying for leaves and outings, lodging maintenance complaints, monitoring fee dues, and adhering to hostel conduct rules.
4. **Hostel Staff**: Operational support personnel (e.g., maintenance technicians, cleaning staff) assigned to a specific hostel. Responsible for viewing assigned maintenance tasks, updating progress, resolving maintenance issues, and recording daily shift tasks.
5. **Security Guard**: Gatekeeping personnel stationed at hostel or campus entry/exit points. Responsible for validating student leave and outing passes, recording actual gate departure and arrival timestamps, and logging visitor entries and exits.
6. **Accountant**: Financial officer responsible for managing hostel fee schedules, logging offline fee receipts, tracking outstanding student balances, proposing fee adjustments, and compiling financial audit summaries.

## 4. Business Rules

The following business rules represent core domain invariants that govern data integrity, authority boundaries, and operational workflows across Nestora:

| Rule ID    | Title                                      | Rule Statement & Enforcement Policy                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| :--------- | :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **BR-001** | Allocation Eligibility Gate                | Only students whose registration record has achieved "Fully Approved" admission status (possessing both University Administrator verification and Hostel Warden verification) are eligible to receive a room or bed allocation.        |
| **BR-002** | Single Active Bed Occupancy                | An individual bed space can be allocated to at most one active student resident at any given time. Concurrent active allocations for the same bed are strictly prohibited.                                                             |
| **BR-003** | Prohibition of Self-Approval               | No user can approve, verify, or authorize their own applications, leave requests, room changes, fee adjustments, or disciplinary appeals.                                                                                              |
| **BR-004** | Hostel-Level Operational Scoping           | Hostel Wardens and Hostel Staff can view, manage, and modify records (students, rooms, leaves, complaints, staff) strictly within their assigned hostel(s). Access to data of unassigned hostels is prohibited.                        |
| **BR-005** | Student Privacy and Isolation              | Resident students can access only their own personal records, room details, fee statements, leave applications, and complaint tickets. Access to other students' private records is prohibited.                                        |
| **BR-006** | Bed Retention During Leave                 | An approved temporary leave or outing does not release, deallocate, or alter the occupancy status of the student's assigned bed. The bed remains reserved for the resident throughout the leave duration.                              |
| **BR-007** | Attendance Modification Auditability       | When a submitted attendance record is modified or corrected, the system must retain the previous value, the modifying user's identity, the modification timestamp, and a mandatory justification note.                                 |
| **BR-008** | Security Role Authority Boundary           | Security Guards are authorized strictly to verify approved passes and log gate movements; they cannot approve, extend, or reject student leave, outing, or visitor requests.                                                           |
| **BR-009** | Fee Structure Activation Authority         | A hostel fee schedule cannot be activated, published, or applied to student ledgers without explicit approval from the University Administrator.                                                                                       |
| **BR-010** | Manual Financial Adjustment Authorization  | Any manual fee adjustment, penalty waiver, or concession proposed by an Accountant requires explicit approval from the University Administrator before altering any student ledger balance.                                            |
| **BR-011** | Emergency Contact Confidentiality          | Student guardian and emergency contact details are confidential and accessible only to authorized roles (University Administrator and the assigned Hostel Warden) during active emergencies or official administrative inquiries.      |
| **BR-012** | Traceability of Critical Actions           | All administrative actions, status mutations, financial approvals, bed allocations, and disciplinary sanctions must generate an unalterable audit trail record capturing user identity, timestamp, action type, and affected entities. |
| **BR-013** | Two-Tier Admission Verification Invariant  | Final student admission approval strictly requires independent sequential approval by both the University Administrator (Tier 1) and the respective Hostel Warden (Tier 2).                                                            |
| **BR-014** | Gender-Categorized Accommodation Invariant | Students can be allocated only to hostels matching their verified gender classification (i.e., male students strictly to Boys' Hostels, female students strictly to Girls' Hostels).                                                   |
| **BR-015** | Mandatory Notification Invariant           | Critical system notices, emergency alerts, security warnings, and urgent disciplinary summons cannot be disabled or opted out of by any user.                                                                                          |
| **BR-016** | Incident Resolution Authority Boundary     | An emergency incident can only be marked as resolved and closed by a Hostel Warden or University Administrator; neither reporting users nor operational staff can close an active incident.                                            |

## 5. Open Questions and Unresolved Scope Decisions

The following items are identified as open questions requiring clarification during subsequent design phases:

1. **Warden Multi-Hostel Assignment**:
   - _Question_: Can a single Hostel Warden be assigned operational control over multiple hostels simultaneously, or must there be a strict 1:1 mapping between Warden and Hostel?
   - _Status_: Open for institutional policy clarification.
2. **Extended Leave Escalation Threshold**:
   - _Question_: What is the specific duration threshold (e.g., leaves exceeding 7 or 14 calendar days) after which student leave requests must be escalated from the Hostel Warden to the University Administrator?
   - _Status_: Open for policy definition.
3. **Fee Structure Uniformity**:
   - _Question_: Should fee schedules be uniform university-wide for a given room configuration, or should the system permit hostel-specific fee differentials based on specialized hostel amenities?
   - _Status_: Open for administrative decision.
4. **Attendance Roster Execution Duties**:
   - _Question_: What is the precise operational boundary between Hostel Wardens and Hostel Staff regarding daily night attendance—can staff independently sign off on daily rosters, or is Warden countersignature mandatory?
   - _Status_: Open for operational clarification.

> **Consolidated register:** All open items above are tracked as `ODR-001` through `ODR-005` in [10-open-decisions-registry.md](./10-open-decisions-registry.md).
